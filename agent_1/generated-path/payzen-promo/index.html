Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to integrate PayZen?

Most developers integrate our React or Web SDK in under 30 minutes. For no-code platforms, we offer native plugins for Webflow, Shopify, and WordPress.

When do I receive payouts from my sales?

With our Growth Pro plan, payouts are instant (24/7/365) to your bank account or crypto wallet. Starter plans settle within 2 business days.

Is PayZen compliant with PCI-DSS?

Yes! PayZen is certified as a PCI-DSS Level 1 Service Provider. Since our checkout widgets handle sensitive card details directly, you never store raw card data on your servers.

Can I accept cryptocurrency alongside fiat?

Absolutely. You can accept USDC, USDT, and Bitcoin alongside traditional credit cards and Apple Pay, with instant auto-conversion to your preferred local currency if desired.
